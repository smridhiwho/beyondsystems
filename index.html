02   Examples

AI features Web3 products can ship.

These are examples of the types of integrations I help teams design and prototype.

DeFi

Wallet Re-engagement Engine

Like Zomato's "let's skip cooking today" notifications, but for wallets. Uses on chain activity to send personalised prompts that bring users back to positions they forgot about at the right moment.

Example prompt
"Your old liquidity pool now yields 3x more."
RWA

Bloomberg Terminal for Tokenised Assets

A unified dashboard for tokenised markets. Aggregates yields, liquidity depth, redemption windows, and asset performance across tokenised assets.

Investors can query data in plain language such as "Show real estate tokens yielding above 8 percent." This turns fragmented RWA dashboards into a research layer similar to a Bloomberg style terminal.

DAO

Governance Participation Engine

Detects new token holders and explains active governance proposals in plain language. It answers questions about proposals and reminds token holders before voting closes.

Most DAOs have thousands of holders but only around 3 percent voter participation. This is often assumed to be a community problem. In many cases it is an automation problem.
